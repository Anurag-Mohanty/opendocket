Does the system present users with clear, accessible, and comprehensive privacy notices at the point of data collection, and does the organization maintain a privacy policy that meets GDPR transparency requirements?
Remediation
At the point of user data collection, such as via `createUser` defined in `internal/graph/schema.graphqls` and handled by `internal/graph/schema.resolvers.go`, or during the `/api/login` process in `cmd/probo/main.go`, ensure a clear and accessible privacy notice is presented to users. Failure to provide transparent information at the time of collection violates GDPR Article 13 requirements.
