How It Works

1

Point it at a repo

Give OpenDocket a GitHub URL. It clones the repository, runs qualification gates, and detects which regulatory domains apply.

2

It asks legal questions

Based on the domain, OpenDocket loads compliance frameworks. Each contains questions pulled from actual regulatory text — the questions a regulator would ask in an investigation.

3

You get a legal brief

For each question, OpenDocket finds evidence in your code — specific files, line numbers, patterns present or absent — and produces a structured report with risk levels, citations, and remediation. Gemini independently reviews every finding.
